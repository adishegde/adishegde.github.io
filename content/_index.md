I’m currently a PhD student at Johns Hopkins University, advised by [Abhishek Jain](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~abhishek/) and [Matthew Green](https://isi.jhu.edu/~mgreen/).

I'm interested in both theoretical and practical aspects of cryptography. I'm especially drawn to concrete problems that motivate clean theoretical models and new techniques, with the long-term goal of bringing those ideas back to practice.

In summer 2026, I interned at NTT Research with [Elette Boyle](https://cs.idc.ac.il/~elette/). Previously, I visited the Simons Institute in summer 2025 and interned at IRIF with [Geoffroy Couteau](https://geoffroycouteau.github.io/) in summer 2024. I graduated from IIIT Bangalore in 2021, where I was advised by [Ashish Choudhury](https://sites.google.com/view/ashish-choudhury).
