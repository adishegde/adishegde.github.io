I’m currently a PhD student at Johns Hopkins University, advised by [Abhishek Jain](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~abhishek/) and [Matthew Green](https://isi.jhu.edu/~mgreen/).

I'm interested in theoretical and applied cryptography. Much of my work focuses on secure computation, and more recently, on designing primitives with low communication and minimal interaction. I'm also interested in applying cryptography to design systems that balance privacy and accountability.

In summer 2026, I interned at NTT Research with [Elette Boyle](https://cs.idc.ac.il/~elette/).
Previously, I visited the Simons Institute in summer 2025 and interned at IRIF with [Geoffroy Couteau](https://geoffroycouteau.github.io/) in summer 2024.
I graduated from IIIT Bangalore in 2021, where I was advised by [Ashish Choudhury](https://sites.google.com/view/ashish-choudhury).
